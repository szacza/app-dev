# Stranger Things

## About the Series
**Stranger Things** is a science fiction and horror television series created by the Duffer Brothers. It takes place in a small town called Hawkins, where strange supernatural events begin to happen.

## My Favorite Characters
- Eleven
- Mike Wheeler
- Dustin Henderson
- Lucas Sinclair
- Max Mayfield

## Why I Like This Series
I like *Stranger Things* because it combines mystery, friendship, adventure, and suspense. The storyline is interesting, and every episode makes me curious about what will happen next.
